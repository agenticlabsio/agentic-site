## Part 6: AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) Strategy

### The AEO Imperative

- **Gartner predicts** 25% of organic search traffic will shift to AI chatbots by 2026
- **65%+ of searches** now end without a click (zero-click searches)
- **527% increase** in AI-referred sessions Jan-May 2025
- FAQ schema content has **highest citation rates** in AI-generated answers

### AEO Content Principles

1. **Answer first, elaborate second**: Lead with the direct answer, then provide context
2. **One idea per paragraph**: AI "chunks" content by paragraph
3. **Explicit structure**: Use headers, tables, bullet points
4. **Question-answer format**: Match how AI presents information
5. **Fresh content signals**: Include year in titles, update regularly
