# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Enterprise and mid-market decision-makers — operations, finance, and IT leaders in regulated or operationally complex industries (banking, biologics manufacturing, logistics, insurance) — evaluating whether agentic AI can be applied credibly to their workflows. They arrive skeptical of AI hype, time-poor, and accountable to compliance and procurement. Secondary readers: their technical evaluators (engineering leads, CTOs) who vet implementation depth via the blog, case studies, and the live demo suite.

The job to be done: decide whether Agentic Labs is a serious, low-risk partner — then book a call or try the demo suite (demo.agenticlabs.io, `apps/demo`).

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Agentic Labs, an agentic-AI consultancy/implementation firm. The site exists to convert skeptical enterprise buyers by demonstrating competence rather than claiming it: concrete use cases, quantified case studies (one featured at a time), a working demo suite as live proof, and legal/trust pages that pass procurement review. Success = qualified calls booked and demo-suite sessions started.

Note on the monorepo: `apps/demo` is the Ledger demo suite — a product-register surface used as sales evidence. When working inside `apps/demo`, override the register to `product` for that task; it shares this brand system (Ledger tokens, `design.md`).

## Brand Personality

Measured, expert, understated. Confidence expressed through calm: hairline rules, whitespace, classical serif display type, one racing-green accent. The voice explains rather than sells; numbers and specifics over adjectives. Emotionally, the site should evoke the trust you'd place in a senior advisor — never the excitement of a launch video.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI-startup SaaS**: purple/violet gradients, glassmorphism, sparkles, robot/brain imagery, "revolutionize your workflow" hype copy, hero-metric templates.
- **Big-consultancy corporate**: Accenture/Deloitte-style stock photography, handshake imagery, vague capability language, walls of same-sized cards.
- Previously retired by design (do not reintroduce): dark mode/theme toggle, entry animations, homepage testimonials, gradients and decorative glows.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't claim** — proof surfaces (case studies, demo suite, real numbers) carry the argument; copy stays declarative and specific.
2. **Restraint is the flex** — one accent, hairline rules, whitespace. If an element shouts, it's off-brand.
3. **Pass procurement** — every page should survive a compliance officer's read: trust/legal pages current, claims quantified, no unverifiable superlatives.
4. **The demo is the CTA** — routes and sections should funnel toward the demo suite and booking a call, not toward more marketing.
5. **Editorial, not templated** — pages read like a well-set publication (Ledger system), not a SaaS page builder.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast on the ivory canvas; keyboard navigable throughout; `prefers-reduced-motion` honored (the site deliberately ships minimal motion — entry animations were removed). Light-mode-only is an intentional brand decision, not an oversight.
